Start with the room host.

This room combines lighting, sensing and research equipment. This website introduces those capabilities; the cabinet panel remains the control surface.

For a demonstration

Ask the host to show warm/cool lighting and the room's existing colour scenes. Coordinate before operating blinds, demos or sensing systems.

For an experiment

Agree who owns each device and how data will be recorded. A panel timer or listed service does not mean sensors are recording.

If something looks wrong

Page connection lost

The browser cannot fetch the status host. Check that your device is on SCR_AP and the touch-panel Pi is reachable. Old readings remain dated.

Room connection unavailable

The status host cannot read the room computer's service list. This is separate from browser connectivity. It does not establish whether a physical device has failed.

Service not listed

The expected interface names were absent from the last successful list. Coordinate with staff before restarting services; installed room launchers can replace whole sessions.

Earlier low voltage

The Pi reports a warning since boot, with no current warning at the sample time. A current low-voltage warning needs attention to the supply, cable and connector. Do not reboot just to hide it.

How this view works

The touch-panel Pi hosts the page on the room LAN. It keeps a cached read-only summary. Outdoor weather comes from Open-Meteo model data, independently of room sensors. Diagnostics shows registration and host information; device commands and staff control are future work.
